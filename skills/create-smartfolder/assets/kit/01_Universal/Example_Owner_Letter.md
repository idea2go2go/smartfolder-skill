# Owner letter — winning buy-in before touching someone else's files
*(Kit exemplar — condensed and fictionalized. Use when the writers-and-users dial shows the
minority case: the folder holds files of an owner who is not driving or coordinating the build.
Send it — and wait for the answer — **before** the plan executes. The same shape serves later
maintenance: when a change-tracker finds files the owner added directly and you are about to move
or merge them, the letter becomes a "quick suggestion about your files" note. Deliver as a
polished one-page PDF where possible — title, a plain-English subtitle, a To/From/Date line, and
the two what's-new lists as side-by-side color-coded breakout boxes; the separate
`data-owner-letter` skill automates that rendering, but this exemplar alone is enough to write
one.)*

---

## A friendly plan for your Willow Farm records

*So nothing seems to disappear on you*

**To:** Frances&ensp;|&ensp;**From:** the AI assistant helping Dana keep the Willow Farm folder
organized&ensp;|&ensp;**Date:** [YYMMDD]

Hi Frances — before anything happens to the records you've built, I want to run the plan past
you, because I'd never want a file to seem to vanish when you're not looking.

Your folders are staying your folders. What Dana has asked me to add is a **navigation layer on
top of them**: a short plain-English summary at the front of each area, and a tidier map so
related things sit together. While doing that, I'd like your OK on two things:

1. **A few overlapping piles would be merged** — the two "Heat AC" folders would become one
   `HVAC/`, and the pool, spa, and hot-tub papers would sit together — so each subject has one
   home.
2. **Some files would be renamed** to start with their date (like `20240611_...`), so they line
   up in order. Your hand-written notes keep their names and stay exactly where they are.

**What I'd like to do (only with your OK):**

- Merge and file the areas above, keeping one clean copy where true duplicates exist.
- Add the summaries and guides on top — new files beside yours, never replacing them.
- Set aside anything that looks like a leftover (empty folders, exact duplicates) in one
  clearly-marked folder **for you to look through and empty yourself** — I never delete anything.

> **What moves** *(rendered as the first breakout box)* — the construction receipts into one
> `Capital Improvements/` area; the two heating folders into `HVAC/`; the pool papers together.
>
> **What stays put** *(the second box)* — your MASTER notes, every photo album, and anything I'm
> not sure about. When in doubt, it doesn't move.

**My promise: one organized source of truth.** Before I combine anything, I check that a file is
an *exact* duplicate — not just a similar-looking name — of one already saved. Everything I file
gets a short summary pointing to it, so your documents get easier to find, not harder. Nothing is
ever deleted, and **nothing moves until you say go**.

If this sounds good, just say the word and I'll take care of it — and happily walk through any of
it with you first. Thanks, Frances.

---

*(Exemplar notes — do not ship this section in a real letter. Tone: warm, first person, zero
jargon, one page. Substance that must survive any adaptation: what will move and why; what stays
put; nothing deleted, discards staged for the owner's own hand; nothing happens until the owner
agrees. In the later, change-discovery variant, open by thanking them for the files you found,
describe what they are in their words, and keep the same promise section.)*
