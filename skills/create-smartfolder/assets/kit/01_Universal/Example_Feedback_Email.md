# Feedback email — the anonymized usage draft, composed only on the owner's acceptance
*(Kit exemplar — the statistics-heavy developer-feedback email. **Nothing is ever composed
unasked:** the offer is made at build close-out and on the milestone schedule the deposited
`VERSION_BASELINE.md` carries (1, 3, and 12 months after build, then every 12), and the offer
names its cost — "Depending on your repository size, this could take 5 to 10 minutes." Only on a
yes does a session compose the folder's own draft from this exemplar — opened first, every number
replaced from the folder's real state — and file it beside the recorded profile as
`YYMMDD_Draft_FeedbackEmail.md`. **The owner reviews and sends; the skill and its deposits never
send anything.** Privacy is structural: pattern-level statistics only — never file contents,
names, paths, people, or subject matter — and the profile rationales are paraphrased with
structural nouns (files, chapters, writers, threads), never domain nouns.)*
