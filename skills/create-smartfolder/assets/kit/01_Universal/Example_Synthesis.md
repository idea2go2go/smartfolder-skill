## The pattern the files reveal (and no single file states)

**The loop-field cylinder must be topped up every May.** The service records show the ground-floor
zone going offline in early summer of 2024 *and* 2026 — both times traced to low water in the 4-ft
black cylinder beside the upstairs unit. The pipe shrinks in winter and expands in summer, so a
level that looks fine in March fails in June. The fix is trivial (the top unscrews; add water; no
pressure involved) — the pattern, not the procedure, is what took two failures to see.
